About Our Club

Discover the story, mission, and journey of Delaware Rhinos

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Delaware Rhinos was established to create a passionate sports community representing Nepali heritage while building strong athletic roots in Delaware. What began as a group of friends sharing a passion for cricket has evolved into a structured community sports organization focused on teamwork, excellence, and brotherhood.

Our Mission

Cricket Development

Fostering talent, promoting high sportsmanship, and offering competitive opportunities in regional cricket leagues.

Health & Brotherhood

Encouraging active physical wellness, mental fitness, and lifelong friendships off the pitch.
